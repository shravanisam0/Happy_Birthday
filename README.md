# Happy_Birthday
# 🎂 Birthday Website

A personalized, interactive birthday website created for my best friend.  
Built with **HTML, CSS, JavaScript**, and dynamic styling features to make the celebration feel lively and memorable.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Home Page**  
  Welcomes visitors with a festive introduction and sets the tone for the celebration.

- **Memories Page**  
  Showcases our cherished moments together with photos, captions, and interactive styling.

- **Message Page**  
  Contains a heartfelt letter written by me to my friend.

- **Reason Page**  
  Highlights the reasons why she is my *bestest friend*, presented in a warm and creative layout.

- **Gift Page**  
  Displays a special digital gift designed just for her.

- **Celebration Page**  
  A fun, dynamic space with animations and effects to celebrate her birthday.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Structure and content
- **CSS3** – Styling, layout, and animations
- **JavaScript (ES6)** – Interactivity
